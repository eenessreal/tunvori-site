Delete your Tunvori account

App: Tunvori · Developer: Enes Emir Yılmaz · Last updated: 21 October 2026

You can delete your Tunvori account and all data linked to it at any time. The quickest way is in the app; if you can't access the app, you can also request deletion by email.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to Tunvori and go to Settings › Account › Delete my account.
  2. Read what will be deleted and tap Delete permanently.
  3. Your account and the data below are deleted from our servers immediately and permanently, and you are signed out.

Delete by email

If you can't access the app (for example, you changed phones or uninstalled it):

  1. From the email address registered to your Tunvori account, send an email with the subject "Account deletion request" to tunvoriapp@tunvori.com. Include your username. If you sign in with Google, use your Google account's email address.
  2. If needed, we reply to confirm that the request comes from the account owner. If you write from a different address, we may ask for more information to verify it.
  3. Your account and the data below are permanently deleted within 30 days at the latest, and we let you know by email when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account details: email address, password and Google sign-in link
  • Your profile: username, display name, bio, role, location and profile photo
  • Your posts and the photos in them
  • Your comments, likes, saved posts and follow relationships
  • The reports you sent, the list of accounts you blocked and your record of accepting the terms
  • Your session records (the IP address and app information of the devices you signed in on)

What is kept

  • Unless the law requires it, we keep no personal data related to your account.
  • Technical access logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP addresses) are deleted automatically when their short retention period ends.
  • Preferences, favorites and your repertoire live on your phone, not on our server; they are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Can I delete only some of my data?

Yes. In the app you can delete your posts and comments, undo likes and saves, unfollow people and change your profile in the Edit Profile screen. If you want anything else deleted, just write to the same address.

Deleting your account does not stop you from using Tunvori's music tools: the tuner, metronome, chords and other tools work without an account.
