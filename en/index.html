Everything you need while you play

The music tools work without an account. When you want to join the community, sign in with your email or your Google account.

Tuner

Listens to your string through the microphone and shows the note and how far off it is. Standard, Drop D, Open G and more, with A4 calibration.

Metronome

Drift-free timing, an accented first beat, time signatures from 2/4 to 6/8 and tap tempo.

Chord library

Open, barre, seventh, sus and jazz chords with fingered diagrams, the notes of each chord and a "Listen" button.

Transpose and capo

Move a progression to any key and find the one that suits your voice; the capo finder shows the easiest fret.

Chord change trainer

Loops a progression with the metronome, shows the next chord ahead of time and keeps track of your daily practice.

Songs

Song sheets with chords, auto-scroll and favorites.

Repertoire

Keep your transposed progressions, saved chords and favorite songs in one place.

Community

Share chord progressions and photos; like, comment and follow other musicians.

Straight talk about your data

The details are in the privacy policy; in short:

No adsNo ads and no analytics tools that track you. Your data is never sold.

The microphone stays on your phoneThe tuner processes sound on your device in real time; nothing is recorded or sent.

Works without an accountThe tuner, metronome, chords and other tools need no account.

Deleted when you askYou can have your account and data deleted at any time.
