Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This policy explains how your personal data is processed when you use the Tunvori mobile app (Android and iOS). The Turkish version of this page also serves as the information notice required by Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

In short: We use no ads or tracking tools and we do not sell your data. The music tools work without an account. The tuner processes microphone sound only on your phone. If you create an account, your profile and posts are stored on our server so that we can provide the service.

1. Who is responsible

Tunvori is developed by the independent developer Enes Emir Yılmaz, who is the data controller.
Contact: tunvoriapp@tunvori.com

2. What data we process

If you create an account

  • Account details: your email address and password. Your password is never stored in plain text; the authentication service keeps only a one-way hash, which we cannot read.
  • If you sign in with Google: the name, email address and Google account ID that Google shares with us.
  • Profile: your username, display name and, if you choose, your bio, profile photo, musician role (e.g. "Guitarist") and location. The location is only the text you type; we never access your device's location.
  • Your content: your posts (text, chord progression, tuning, tags, photo, visibility and comment settings) and your comments.
  • Your interactions: your likes, saved posts, the people you follow and who follow you, and the reports you send with their reasons.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider briefly keeps standard access logs such as IP address and request time for security and operation.

Data that stays only on your device

Your theme, language and color palette preferences; favorite songs and saved chords; the progressions in your repertoire; your practice time and streak; metronome and tuner settings; your post draft. These are never sent to our server and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

App permissions

  • Microphone: used only while the tuner screen is open. Sound is analyzed on your phone in real time to detect the note; it is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Photos: only the photo you pick is accessed. It is uploaded to our server when you share it as your profile photo or in a post.
  • Internet: for account and community features.

Data is collected electronically through the forms in the app and your actions. It is not used for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making.

4. Who can see your data

  • Your profile, posts and comments are shown only to signed-in Tunvori users. A post shared with "Followers" visibility is visible only to people who follow you.
  • Profile and post photos are stored at a long, unguessable web address so that the app can display them. Anyone who has that address can open the photo; when you delete the photo or the post, the address stops working.
  • Only you can see the posts you save. The reports you send are visible to you and to us for review; the reported person is never told who reported them.

5. Who we share data with

Your data is never sold or shared for advertising. We rely only on these service providers to run Tunvori:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and file storage. The servers are in the European Union, in the Frankfurt (Germany) region.
  • Google: for authentication if you use "Sign in with Google"; verification and password reset emails are also sent through Google Workspace.
  • Google Play and the Apple App Store: to let you download and update the app. These stores operate under their own privacy policies.

Because these providers' servers are located outside Turkey, your data is transferred abroad in order to provide the service. Data may also be disclosed to competent public authorities when the law requires it, to the extent they request.

6. How long we keep data

  • Your account, profile, content and interactions are kept while your account is open.
  • When you remove a post, comment or like, it is deleted from the server right away.
  • When you have your account deleted, this data is permanently deleted within 30 days at the latest (see Delete Account).
  • Technical access logs are deleted automatically when the hosting provider's short retention period ends.

7. Security

All connections between the app and the server are encrypted (HTTPS). The database enforces row-level access rules: each user can change only their own data, and fields such as like and follower counts cannot be written by the app directly. No system is completely risk-free, though. Never share your password.

8. Children

Tunvori is not designed for children under 13, and children under 13 should not create an account. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we delete the account and its data. You can let us know by email.

9. Your rights

You have the right to learn whether your data is processed and request information about it; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; to know the third parties it is transferred to in Turkey or abroad; to ask for incorrect or incomplete data to be corrected; to ask for your data to be deleted under the conditions of the law; to ask that corrections and deletions be notified to those third parties; to object to a decision against you made solely by automated systems; and to claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

You can change your profile in the app's Edit Profile screen and delete your posts and comments in the app. For anything else, write to tunvoriapp@tunvori.com from the email address on your account. We answer free of charge within 30 days at the latest.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version is always on this page, and the effective date above changes with it.
